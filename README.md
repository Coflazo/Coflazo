**Cagan Oflazoglu** · econometrics and machine learning · Amsterdam

Recent work: a gravity model of the 2025 US tariffs on realized customs data, a parking search that checks whether your car fits the bay, and an engine that scores early-stage founders against a fund's thesis.

### Selected work

- **[Was-Liberation-a-Delusion](https://github.com/Coflazo/Was-Liberation-a-Delusion)**: BSc Economics thesis, University of Amsterdam (2026). PPML gravity model testing whether the 2025 tariffs cut US imports or moved them from China to other foreign suppliers. Thesis PDF and full replication pipeline. Python.
- **[CamToParkingSlot](https://github.com/Coflazo/CamToParkingSlot)**: parking search that matches RDW vehicle dimensions against Amsterdam's 210,247 surveyed bays and reads live street cameras for free spaces. Python, C++.
- **[VC-Alpha-Signal-Finder](https://github.com/Coflazo/VC-Alpha-Signal-Finder)**: evaluation engine for early-stage venture. Reads seven public sources, builds founder and company dossiers, and scores each against a plain-English thesis with the evidence attached. Python.
- **[Octant-AI](https://github.com/Coflazo/Octant-AI)**: agent that turns an investment thesis into a financial model and a written research report. Python.
- **[congressmAIn](https://github.com/Coflazo/congressmAIn)**: plain-language summaries of Dutch council meetings, with decisions and votes. Winner, AISO × AI020 Gov Tech Hackathon; invited to demo with the Amsterdam Municipality. Python, TypeScript.
- **[calibrcv-cli](https://github.com/Coflazo/calibrcv-cli)**: resume optimizer that runs in the terminal, offline through Ollama, with LaTeX output and ATS scoring. JavaScript.
